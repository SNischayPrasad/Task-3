# Buyer Persona – FitBowl Meal Subscription

**Digital Marketing Track | Task: Create a Buyer Persona**
Author: Sadhanala Nischay Prasad

## Objective
Understand customer segmentation and audience targeting by building a detailed buyer persona for a product.

## Product
**FitBowl** – a healthy, high-protein meal subscription for working professionals in Hyderabad's IT corridor (HITEC City, Gachibowli, Madhapur, Kondapur). Plans from ₹2,999/month.

## Persona: "Priya the Busy Professional"
27-year-old software engineer in Gachibowli who wants to eat healthy but has no time to cook and relies on oily delivery food.

## Deliverables covered

| --- | --- |
| Complete buyer persona | Persona Snapshot, Psychographics and Interests |
| Customer pain points | Customer Pain Points |

| Problem → solution link | How FitBowl Solves Her Problems |
| Targeting | Targeting and Messaging |

## Key insights
- The persona is a specific segment (24–34, salaried tech professionals, Hyderabad), not "everyone".
- Instagram drives discovery; WhatsApp and the app drive conversion and retention.
- A low-risk trial pack is the main conversion lever; menu variety and on-time delivery prevent churn.
